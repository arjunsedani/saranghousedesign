Sections and Movement

A-A at Z 2200: 1948.4 open floor + 2100 bed + 101.6 headboard = 4150. Wardrobe-to-bed: 640 nominal before handles. Full 508 extension now leaves 160 to the bed including enlarged grips, not standing space. Foot-side approach does not guarantee every bay is accessible. Trial reach and drawer use beside the bed. Doors open schematically to 90 degrees; hardware clearances need approval. The floating dresser retains its 400 carcass depth, plus the new 23-projecting handle; bedside handle also projects 23 beyond its front. Keep the chair clear of the dresser drawer.

All wardrobe drawers: 18 pairs of requested 20-inch-class Hettich Quadro undermount channels. Exact 20 in = 508 mm, used as the planning envelope; confirm series, nominal length, drawer-box dimensions, required cabinet depth, fixing, bottom thickness, load rating and clips. A 500 mm SKU is not exactly 508 mm. Hidden drawer rear clearance is approximately 22 in this simplified model. Taller fronts do not establish usable internal height or approved bottom machining. No drilling or cutting dimensions are certified.

Factory hold: confirm larger-grip clearance, personal reach, the slim 150 pocket and high side rail, 571.5-wide pull-down hardware, 640 aisle, anchors and laminate build-up. The register is measured model geometry, not an approved cutting, hinge-boring or edging release.
