H1 & N1 / 2-to-4 Ply CNC Cutout, Fire-Safe Laminate & Mini-Light Routing Schedule

To eliminate expensive PU paint while ensuring visible panel joints look architectural and candle/spotlight heat is safely managed, the entire `2,431.2 mm` feature wall (`Z = 950..3,381.2 mm`, `X = 4,048.4..4,150.0 mm`, `101.6 mm / 4 in` total depth) is engineered as a 3-layer dry-assembled plywood system finished in 1.0 mm Fire-Retardant (FR Grade) Matte Stucco/Stone Textured Laminate (`#d8cfc2`) with intentional 3 mm shadow-line bevels at `Z = 1200, 1927, and 2654 mm`:

  • Layer 1 — Rear Backing Ply (`X = 4132..4150 mm`, `18 mm` BWR/FR Plywood): Screwed directly to the masonry wall; forms the illuminated rear surface of all 7 arched niches and carries the `10 x 8 mm` vertical CNC wire-routing grooves for the low-voltage 12V/24V LED drivers (housed in a ventilated service box behind the removable bedside drawer or bed-rest cushion).
  • Layer 2 — Internal Framing Battens & Arch Ribs (`X = 4066.4..4132 mm`, `65.6 mm` depth): Rip-cut from `18 mm` plywood strips (`65.6 mm` wide) with kerf-bent `6 mm` flexi-ply arch hoods lined with `1.0 mm` FR laminate + `2 mm` aluminum heat-reflector tape above each mini-spotlight and candle zone.
  • Layer 3 — Front CNC-Routed Face Panels (`X = 4048.4..4066.4 mm`, `18 mm` BWR/FR Plywood): 4 modular vertical panels (`N1-PNL1`, `H1-PNL2`, `H1-PNL3`, `H1-PNL4`) that nest onto just 2 standard 8x4 ft (`2440 x 1220 mm`) plywood sheets (or 4 sheets total including the rear backing panels and internal ribs).Combined with the `18 mm` face panel + `65.6 mm` cavity, each arched niche provides a realistic 83.6 mm (~3.3 in) clear shelf depth.
